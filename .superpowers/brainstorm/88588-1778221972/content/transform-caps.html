What can the handles do?

How much transform power does the video layer need?

✥
drag to move
no resize handles

A — Move only

Drag the selected video to reposition it. Scale stays at 100% (fills the frame). Simple — great for repositioning within a fixed-size canvas.

drag body to move
↘ corner = scale

B — Move + scale

Drag the body to move. Drag any corner handle to scale (zoom in/out uniformly). Scale is stored as a multiplier (e.g. 1.5× = 150% zoom). Useful for punch-in shots or fitting letterbox footage.

move + scale + rotate

C — Move + scale + rotate

Full transform: move, scale, and rotate. Rotation handle above the bounding box. Rotation is stored in degrees. Most powerful, but adds complexity to rendering and export.
