Can the video overflow the canvas?

What happens when you drag the video toward the edge?

A — Stays inside (bounded)
can't go further →
hits invisible wall at canvas edge
Clamps to canvas bounds. Video can't extend beyond the frame edge. Safe, predictable — content is always fully visible.
B — Can overflow (like Figma/Canva)
dragged past edge →
cropped at canvas edge, moves freely beyond
Video can move past the canvas edge — cropped by the frame like a real compositor. Useful for pan-in/out effects, cinematic framing adjustments.

Note: the exported video always uses the canvas bounds as the crop frame — overflow just means part of the video is intentionally off-screen.
