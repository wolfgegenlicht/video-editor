Three different interaction models for repositioning the video layer

A — Click & drag video directly

Click anywhere on the video to grab it, drag to reposition. No selection step — the video is always draggable. Captions float independently on their own layer above.

VIDEO
✓ selected

B — Click to select, handles to transform

Click the video to select it — shows a bounding box with corner/edge handles. Drag the box to move; drag corners to resize/scale. Click outside to deselect. Like Figma/Canva.
