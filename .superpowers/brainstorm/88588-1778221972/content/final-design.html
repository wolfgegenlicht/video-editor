Interaction summary

  • Click video — selects it, shows dashed bounding box + handles. Auto-opens Properties panel.
  • Drag body — moves X/Y (percentage of canvas)
  • Drag corner handle — uniform scale from center
  • Drag rotation handle (purple, above box) — rotates in degrees
  • Click outside — deselects
  • Properties panel — numeric X/Y/Scale/Rotation inputs, Reset button
  • Captions — unaffected, always on their own layer above
  • Export — FFmpeg applies the transform (translate + scale + rotate) per clip
