How it works technically

  • The canvas container keeps overflow: hidden — clips the video rendering at the frame edge
  • The selection handles live in a sibling div on the outer wrapper — not inside the clipped container — so they're never cut off
  • Both divs share the same transform values, so handles stay perfectly aligned with the (partially hidden) video
  • The outer wrapper has no overflow clipping — handles can appear anywhere around the canvas
